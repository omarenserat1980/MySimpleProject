# BRAIN-ASIC-001 — Zero-Budget Ownership and Market-Valuation Policy

**Owner intent:** The project owner authorizes zero-budget development only. A zero development budget is not a zero valuation of a real product.

## Policy
1. Authorized spend is 0 JOD. No purchase, paid service, shipping commitment, or financial obligation may be made without the owner's explicit authorization.
2. Keep these values separate in all code, reports, and dashboards:
   - `development_budget_jod`: 0 under the current authorization
   - `physical_inventory_count_verified`: count only physically observed items with evidence
   - `estimated_market_value`: a sourced estimate for a real identified asset, with currency, valuation date, condition, model/revision, and source links
   - `asking_price`: seller's intended listing price; not proof of market value or a completed sale
   - `sale_price_realized`: amount from a documented completed transaction
   - `revenue_received`: amount actually received and reconciled
   - `net_profit`: calculated only after documented transaction costs, fees, refunds, tax obligations where applicable, and revenue received
3. Simulated components and designs have no recorded physical inventory value. They may have a separate hypothetical product/IP valuation only if explicitly labeled as an estimate and supported by a defensible method; never treat it as cash or realized revenue.
4. Do not claim ownership of a physical part until possession/rights are evidenced. Legal ownership and IP rights depend on applicable law, contracts, and third-party licenses.
5. Market valuation requires comparable real listings or completed transactions for the same model/revision and similar condition. Record the date and URLs. Asking prices alone do not prove a sale price.
6. No automatic purchases, listing, sale, transfer, or financial execution. Require owner approval before any binding action.
7. Reports must label simulated, estimated, listed, sold, and paid states distinctly. CI success is not commercial revenue evidence.
