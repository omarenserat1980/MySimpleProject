# BRAIN-ASIC-001 — Jordan Zero-Budget Component Sourcing Leads

**Purpose:** Potential contacts to ask whether they have an unwanted mining hashboard/chip available for a no-cost donation or reuse. These are leads, not confirmed donors. No one has agreed to donate.

**Budget:** 0 JOD total, including delivery. Do not accept paid pickup, shipping, inspection, or processing.

## Priority 1: Ask an e-waste recycler before equipment is processed

### E-TAFKEEK (electronic-waste recycling)
- Website: https://e-tafkeek.com/
- Public site describes recovery/reuse of used computers and electronic boards.
- Ask only whether a discarded Bitcoin-mining hashboard or ASIC chip can be released at no cost for educational/open-source research. The site does not promise ASIC inventory or free donations.
- Do not take material from a recycling stream without explicit permission and clear transfer of ownership.

### Jordan Ministry of Environment — e-waste guidance
- Official page: https://www.moenv.gov.jo/AR/ListDetails/مشاريع-الوزارة/35/5
- The Ministry page says to contact the nearest Environment Directorate to determine an appropriate e-waste collection point. It lists the central region contact as 05-560113 and Zarqa Directorate as 05-3656680.
- Collection points are for proper disposal, not necessarily donation. Ask for guidance to an authorized reuse/donation program; do not remove items from a collection point without permission.

### Official e-waste collection locations
- Ministry page: https://www.moenv.gov.jo/Ar/NewsDetails/مواقع_تجميع_النفايات_الكهربائية_و_الالكترونية
- The published list includes sites in Amman such as the Ministry of Environment and several universities/parks. Confirm current operation and permissions before visiting. These sites are not confirmed sources of ASIC parts.

## Evidence and next actions
- Record contact attempts only after a person actually sends them.
- Record a response as CONTACTED, DECLINED, POSSIBLE_LEAD, or DONATION_CONFIRMED with date and source.
- Do not change physical inventory until the item is received and inspected.
- Do not publish names or personal contact details of individual donors without consent.
- Never record a lead as a donor until a specific item is offered and transfer conditions are confirmed.
- A generic computer PCB or recycled electronics item is not a mining ASIC. Identify markings and function before classifying it.
