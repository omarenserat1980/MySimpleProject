# Brain Marketing Department

## Purpose
A governed marketing system for Electronic Brain that researches markets, prepares content and campaigns, manages approvals, publishes through connected channels, and measures outcomes.

## Pipeline
Research → Audience → Offer → Content → Approval → Publish → Measure → Learn → Improve

## Core modules
- Market Research
- Audience & ICP
- Offers and service catalog
- Content Engine
- SEO Intelligence
- Campaign Manager
- Social Publishing
- Lead/CRM intake
- Approval Gate
- Marketing Analytics
- Experiment Ledger
- Audit Log

## Governance
No external outreach, paid advertising, or irreversible publication is performed without the required authorization. Every campaign stores owner, objective, audience, channel, content version, approval state, timestamps, and outcome evidence.

## Initial KPIs
- qualified visits
- contact/form submissions
- qualified leads
- conversion rate
- cost per qualified lead when paid campaigns exist
- content engagement
- organic search impressions/clicks
- lead-to-customer progression

## First launch
1. Publish the Electronic Brain promotional site.
2. Establish official brand messaging.
3. Create the first 30-day organic content plan.
4. Connect analytics.
5. Establish lead intake and CRM states.
6. Prepare channel-specific content for review.
7. Measure results before considering paid acquisition.
