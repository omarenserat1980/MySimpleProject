## 4. What would count as legal proof in Jordan?

The relevant official routes depend on the selected legal form:

- Companies Control Department: https://www.ccd.gov.jo/Default/Ar/ — provides company registration, legal filings, company certificates and company-status inquiry.
- Company status inquiry: https://www.ccd.gov.jo/AR/Pages/استعلام_الشركات — allows lookup of basic data for registered companies.
- Ministry of Industry, Trade and Supply: https://mit.gov.jo/Ar/Pages/المؤسسات_الفردية_والأسماء_التجارية — covers individual establishments and trade names.

A real evidence packet should contain, as applicable:
1. An authentic registration certificate or official trade-register certificate.
2. Official confirmation of legal name, number, legal form and actual registration date.
3. Founding contract/bylaws and ownership details where applicable.
4. Any required business licences and activity approvals.
5. Tax registration and compliance evidence where required.
6. A trademark certificate only if registration is actually granted.
7. Authentic financial records for any capital, payments or revenue claims.

The official Companies Law text describes formation documents and registration procedures for relevant company forms. The precise route, current rules and costs must be confirmed with the competent authority or a qualified Jordanian adviser before filing.
