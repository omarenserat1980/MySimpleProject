## 4. Revenue realization gate

A lead, quote, sandbox payment, simulated order, mining estimate, or transaction ID alone is not realized revenue.

Record each stage separately:
LEAD -> CUSTOMER CONFIRMED -> SCOPE ACCEPTED -> PAYMENT VERIFIED -> SERVICE DELIVERED -> CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE -> COSTS VERIFIED -> NET RESULT CALCULATED.

Revenue is recognized only when supported by authentic payment evidence and the relevant delivery records. Net profit must subtract actual fees, refunds, chargebacks, infrastructure, electricity, taxes and other costs as applicable. Never create fictitious orders, customer identities, payment confirmations, mining shares, payouts, or profits.

Do not collect or move customer funds, sign agreements, submit legal declarations, or accept binding obligations autonomously. These require the owner's explicit approval and any required legal/accounting review.
