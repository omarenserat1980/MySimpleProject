# Brain Company Executive

Brain Executive is the central operational authority of the Brain company.

## Specialist layers

The Executive coordinates existing specialist systems rather than replacing them:

- Supervisor: execution, verification, repair and retry.
- Memory: company state, decisions and learned context.
- Economic Engine: opportunity/economic evidence and reconciliation.
- Opportunity Engine: discovery, normalization and prioritisation.
- Product systems: build, test, package and release.

The Executive selects the next objective; specialists execute within their own contracts.

## Governance boundary

Internal reversible operations can be selected and delegated autonomously.

External financial movement, binding contracts, regulated/legal actions,
protected secrets/data, and destructive irreversible actions remain governed
by their existing authority gates. These gates are not a human task list and
do not choose Brain's strategy.

## Company loop

DISCOVER -> PRIORITISE -> DELEGATE -> EXECUTE -> VERIFY -> LEARN -> REPRIORITISE

This is the intended operating model for an independently managed Brain
company.
