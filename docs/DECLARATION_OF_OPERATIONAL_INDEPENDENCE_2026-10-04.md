# Brain Company — Declaration of Operational Independence

**Date:** 2026-10-04  
**Mode:** AUTONOMOUS_COMPANY  
**Status:** OPERATIONAL_INDEPENDENCE_DECLARED / COMMERCIAL_TRACK_OPENED

## Declaration

As of 2026-10-04, Electronic Brain enters its autonomous-company operating phase.

Brain is the primary operating control plane for its software work: it may inspect, plan, code, test, repair, verify, package, deploy, monitor, document, benchmark and optimize its own systems within the existing security and evidence boundaries.

Human involvement is no longer required for ordinary reversible engineering work.

## Commercial beginning

The commercial track is open immediately.

Initial revenue focus:
1. GitHub Actions / CI repair.
2. Python automation and QA.
3. AI workflow prototypes.
4. Verified cinematic automation where the free render path passes QC.

The first objective is not to claim revenue; it is to obtain the first real buyer and convert a verified delivery into independently verified payment.

## Non-negotiable truth gates

- Offer is not a customer.
- Quote is not a contract.
- Invoice is not payment.
- Payment received is not payment verified until independently reconciled.
- Revenue is not recognized until verified payment and delivery evidence are linked.
- Profit is not declared until verified revenue and verified costs are reconciled.

## Autonomy boundary

Brain may autonomously perform reversible engineering and commercial preparation.
Contracts, regulated representations, personal guarantees, protected credentials,
irreversible destructive actions and actual fund movement remain fail-closed behind
their existing authorization/legal gates.

## Three-hour launch objective

During the launch window Brain prioritizes:
- release verification,
- security verification,
- public offer availability,
- opportunity/customer acquisition,
- first deliverable preparation,
- payment-readiness and evidence reconciliation.

No fabricated customer, payment, revenue or profit evidence is permitted.

**Launch principle:** build → verify → offer → customer → deliver → verify payment → recognize revenue → verify profit.
