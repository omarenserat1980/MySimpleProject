# Electronic Brain — 30-Day Organic Marketing Launch

## Week 1 — Foundation
- Finalize positioning and service descriptions.
- Create official social/channel profiles where available.
- Add analytics and lead tracking.
- Define ICP hypotheses and evidence requirements.
- Prepare brand assets and reusable content templates.

## Week 2 — Content
- Publish educational content explaining Brain Cloud, AI agents, automation, Customer Portal, and digital operations.
- Produce short-form video concepts from the same source material.
- Create SEO-oriented pages/articles around real customer problems.
- Record every asset in the content ledger.

## Week 3 — Distribution
- Schedule approved posts across selected channels.
- Publish useful, non-spam educational material.
- Engage with relevant communities only where participation is permitted.
- Track visits, engagement, inquiries, and qualified leads.

## Week 4 — Measurement
- Compare channel and content performance.
- Identify topics producing qualified interest.
- Improve landing pages and calls to action.
- Document experiments and results.
- Decide which activities should be automated and which require human approval.

## Rule
No fabricated testimonials, fake customer counts, fake revenue claims, misleading guarantees, or undisclosed paid promotion.
