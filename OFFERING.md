## Commercial truth

Offer ≠ customer.
Quote ≠ contract.
Invoice ≠ payment.
Only independently verified payment can become PAYMENT_VERIFIED, and only after delivery/reconciliation can it become REVENUE_REALIZED.

No customer, payment, revenue, or profit is fabricated by Brain.

## Intake

Prospective buyers should provide:
1. desired outcome,
2. current artifact/repository or requirements,
3. acceptance criteria,
4. deadline,
5. preferred communication channel.

Brain may prepare the scope and delivery package. Binding contracts, regulated representations, personal guarantees, and actual fund movement remain behind the existing authorization/legal gates.
