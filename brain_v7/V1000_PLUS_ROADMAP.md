## V2000 — Next Major Milestone
V2000 represents the next architectural milestone after V1000. It is a version target, not a claim of consciousness or human-equivalent intelligence.

Requested development mode:
- prioritize implementation and architecture growth
- defer full validation gates unless explicitly re-enabled
- never report an unimplemented module as completed
- preserve safety boundaries around system execution
