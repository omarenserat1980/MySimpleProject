# YouTube Deep Integrity Layer

## Content identity

Every candidate can carry a deterministic fingerprint derived from its identity, niche, title angle, script fingerprint, and asset fingerprint. The fingerprint prevents accidental duplicate production.

## Experiment identity

Experiments require an explicit hypothesis and variant. Duplicate experiment IDs are collapsed before learning so repeated records cannot manufacture evidence.

## Measurement integrity

Brain must not learn aggressively from tiny samples. The measurement gate requires minimum impressions and views before results become usable for strategy learning.

These thresholds are conservative defaults, not claims about YouTube's own analytics or monetization requirements.

## Financial isolation

Content fingerprints, experiments, impressions, views, watch time, subscribers, RPM estimates, and expected value remain non-financial analytics. None can create confirmed revenue.

## Single-orchestrator rule

These components are pure decision/evidence layers. They do not publish, spawn workers, modify historical measurements, or launch parallel loops.
