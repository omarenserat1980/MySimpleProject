# YouTube Channel Strategy Deep Layer

## Decision hierarchy

1. Channel viability: audience fit, demand, competition, production fit, monetization fit, originality headroom.
2. Video selection: demand, originality, production cost, production risk.
3. Quality gates: technical QC and cinematic QC before PUBLISH_READY.
4. Experimentation: compare measured outcomes rather than intuition.
5. Economic truth: performance metrics are not revenue; payment evidence is required for confirmed revenue.

## Learning policy

The Brain may improve future choices from measured performance, but it must not rewrite historical metrics or financial evidence.

## First channel strategy

Use one Arabic faceless channel initially. Produce a controlled batch of original videos across a narrow niche, then compare experiments using watch-seconds-per-impression as a primary quality signal, followed by views per video and click-through proxy.

Do not create multiple channels or parallel autonomous production loops until the first channel demonstrates stable operational quality and a measurable learning signal.
