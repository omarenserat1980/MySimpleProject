# YouTube Learning and Expansion Gate

Brain must not scale a channel because one video performed well. Expansion requires repeated evidence.

## Gates

- Fewer than 5 measured experiments: CONTINUE.
- Fewer than 5,000 impressions: CONTINUE.
- Five or more experiments with insufficient repeated winners: ITERATE.
- Repeated winning signal after the evidence thresholds: EXPAND.

These thresholds are conservative operating defaults, not claims about YouTube monetization eligibility or guaranteed income.

## Financial separation

Performance metrics remain analytics. They never become confirmed revenue. Confirmed revenue remains controlled by the Economic Control Plane and valid payment evidence.

## Anti-runaway rule

The learning engine is a pure decision layer. It does not spawn workers, publish videos, open new channels, or launch parallel self-healing loops. The existing single orchestrator remains responsible for execution.
