# Brain Master Intelligence Operating Model

Brain combines specialist domains without collapsing them into one unsupported opinion.

## Specialist layers
- Marketing and growth
- Commerce and supply chain
- Finance and investment analysis
- Social impact and NGOs
- Social science
- Political analysis
- Economics
- Defence/security

## Decision sequence
1. Define the question.
2. Identify relevant domains.
3. Collect source-backed evidence.
4. Separate facts from assumptions.
5. Score evidence confidence.
6. Detect material risk and contradictions.
7. Generate scenarios rather than pretending certainty.
8. Select a permission-safe next action.
9. Record the evidence and decision fingerprint.
10. Learn from measured outcomes.

## Action boundary
Research, analysis, simulation, planning and drafting can be automated.
External publication, purchases, ad spend, contracts, account access, fundraising, money movement, political persuasion, and sensitive operational security actions remain permission-gated.

## Truth boundary
Predictions are not facts. Opportunities are not revenue. Targets are not results. Plans are not execution.
