# CL-000003 — First Real Revenue Experiment

## Purpose

Convert a real external prospect into a paid Brain customer without fabricating
customer identity, demand, payment, revenue, or profit.

## Prospect

- ID: CL-000003
- Candidate: Al-Sultan Co. for Industrial Equipment
- Geography: Amman, Jordan
- Segment: industrial equipment / tools / hydraulic / pneumatic / welding
- Current state: PROSPECT
- Source: public company information and official/public web presence
- No customer relationship is claimed until explicit acceptance exists.

## Commercial hypothesis

Brain can create measurable commercial value for an industrial-equipment seller
by improving the path from product intent to qualified quote request.

The initial experiment should focus on ONE product/category only. Do not build a
large website or broad campaign before proving one conversion path.

## Candidate offer

**Industrial Quote Conversion Pilot**

Deliverables:
1. One focused Arabic/English product/category landing experience.
2. Clear product specification/value presentation.
3. Quote/request capture flow.
4. Attribution/evidence for every qualified inquiry.
5. Before/after measurement.
6. Handover report.

The exact product/category and commercial price require customer approval.

## Customer-value measurement

Baseline period:
- record current qualified inquiries for the selected category;
- record current quote requests;
- record current completed orders attributable to that category if the customer
  can provide them;
- record the measurement window and source.

Pilot period:
- qualified inquiries;
- quote requests;
- quotes issued;
- orders confirmed;
- customer-confirmed revenue/gross profit attributable to the pilot.

No modeled uplift is treated as realized customer gain.

## Brain revenue measurement

Brain revenue is a separate ledger:

PROSPECT
 -> OFFER_ACCEPTED
 -> SERVICE_DELIVERED
 -> CUSTOMER_ACCEPTANCE
 -> PAYMENT_VERIFIED
 -> REVENUE_REALIZED

Required payment evidence:
- amount;
- currency;
- payment date;
- payment reference/receipt;
- payer/customer linkage;
- independently verifiable source.

## Profit measurement

PROFIT_VERIFIED requires:
- verified Brain revenue;
- attributable execution costs;
- cost evidence;
- reconciliation showing revenue minus attributable costs.

## Hard safety boundaries

- No invented customer.
- No invented order.
- No invented invoice.
- No invented payment.
- No automatic contract.
- No automatic charging.
- No automatic withdrawal.
- No claim of revenue from a forecast, invoice, lead, click, or green CI run.
- One active request for CL-000003.
- Do not add CL-000003 to the two active execution customers until the
  commercial acceptance and execution scope are explicitly authorized.

## Exit criteria

### Customer validated
Explicit customer acceptance of the offer/order.

### Payment verified
Independent payment evidence matches the accepted order.

### Revenue realized
Payment + delivery evidence are reconciled.

### Profit verified
Revenue + attributable cost evidence are reconciled.

### Experiment failure
If the prospect declines, cannot validate the baseline, or no measurable
commercial path exists, record the failure and do not manufacture a success.

## First outreach constraint

Any outreach must be explicitly authorized before sending. The Brain may
prepare the offer and message, but this record does not authorize sending,
contracting, or charging the prospect.
