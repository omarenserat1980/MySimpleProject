# CL-000003 — Paid Pilot Offer Template

## Offer name

Industrial Quote Conversion Pilot

## Objective

Improve and measure the conversion path for ONE agreed industrial product/category
from product interest to qualified quote request.

## Scope

Brain will provide:

1. Focused Arabic/English product presentation.
2. One measurable quote/request path.
3. Inquiry capture and attribution.
4. Baseline measurement before pilot.
5. Pilot-period measurement.
6. Delivery evidence.
7. End-of-pilot results report.

## Commercial terms

The following MUST be explicitly agreed with the customer before execution:

- selected product/category;
- pilot duration;
- delivery scope;
- acceptance criteria;
- price;
- currency;
- payment timing;
- payment method;
- customer contact/authorized approver.

No value is assumed until these fields are accepted.

## Success measurement

Customer-side:
- baseline qualified inquiries;
- pilot qualified inquiries;
- quote requests;
- quotes issued;
- confirmed orders;
- customer-confirmed attributable revenue/gross profit.

Brain-side:
- agreed service price;
- payment received;
- payment reference;
- delivery evidence;
- acceptance evidence;
- attributable execution costs.

## Revenue gate

Brain revenue remains NOT_REALIZED until:

1. customer acceptance/order is evidenced;
2. service delivery is evidenced;
3. payment is independently verified;
4. payment is reconciled to the accepted commercial scope.

## Profit gate

Profit remains NOT_VERIFIED until:

1. revenue is verified;
2. attributable costs are evidenced;
3. revenue and costs reconcile.

## Customer outcome gate

Customer gain remains UNVERIFIED until the customer confirms the relevant
commercial outcome with evidence.

Clicks, traffic, leads, projections, modeled uplift, or internal test results
are not customer financial gain.

## Authorization boundary

This template authorizes preparation only.

It does NOT authorize:
- contacting the prospect;
- signing a contract;
- accepting terms;
- charging a payment method;
- moving funds;
- purchasing third-party services;
- making financial commitments.

Those actions require explicit authorization and applicable evidence.

## Failure handling

If the prospect declines, does not respond, cannot establish a baseline, or
the pilot cannot produce measurable evidence, record the result as a commercial
experiment outcome. Never convert an unsuccessful experiment into a revenue claim.
