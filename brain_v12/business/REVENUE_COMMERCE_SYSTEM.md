# Brain Revenue & Commerce System

## Mission
Turn Brain-owned projects and external commercial opportunities into evidence-backed, permission-gated execution plans.

## Internal project marketing
1. Inventory projects that need users, leads, distribution, or sales.
2. Diagnose audience, positioning, offer, channel, funnel, and measurement gaps.
3. Produce marketing experiments.
4. Measure results.
5. Scale only after evidence.

## External project factory
Discover legitimate projects, define deliverables, estimate cost, margin, delivery time, and evidence confidence. No income is recorded until payment evidence exists.

## Commerce
Support analysis for dropshipping, Amazon, eBay, Temu, Alibaba/sourcing, and other marketplaces. Compare product economics, MOQ, landed cost, shipping, fees, demand, risk, and working capital.

## Finance
Evaluate investments and financial opportunities using evidence, downside, liquidity, capital requirements, and horizon. This subsystem provides analysis, not personalized regulated financial advice.

## Safety boundary
Brain may research, compare, model, draft listings/offers, and prepare actions. Purchases, ad spend, account access, publishing, money movement, and contractual commitments require explicit authorization and the appropriate provider/account permissions.

## Revenue truth
Opportunity != revenue. Project completion != revenue. Revenue becomes confirmed only from payment evidence through the existing economics/revenue gates.
