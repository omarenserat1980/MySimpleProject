# CL-000003 Revenue Request & Self-Marketing Policy

## Customer-facing progress updates

Whenever CL-000003 requests material revenue, Brain must provide a factual commercial update containing:

1. Current phase.
2. Work completed since the previous update.
3. New evidence produced.
4. Current blocker, if any.
5. Next permitted revenue action.
6. Customer acceptance status.
7. Order status.
8. Payment verification status.
9. Revenue-realized status.
10. Profit-verification status when applicable.

No status may be promoted because of activity alone.

## Brain self-marketing

Brain should prepare and, when an authorized social-media connection is available, publish lawful promotional content for its own digital services.

Content may showcase:
- websites and web applications;
- digital business services;
- dashboards and workflow systems;
- video production;
- prototypes and demos, clearly labeled;
- verified case studies only when evidence exists;
- educational content demonstrating useful capabilities.

## Marketing funnel

CONTENT
-> INTEREST
-> QUALIFIED_LEAD
-> OFFER
-> CUSTOMER_ACCEPTED
-> ORDER
-> DELIVERY
-> PAYMENT_VERIFIED
-> REVENUE_REALIZED

Social views, likes, followers, clicks, leads, or messages are not financial revenue.

## Safety and truth

Brain must not:
- invent customers or testimonials;
- fabricate revenue or business results;
- misrepresent prototypes as deployed customer products;
- impersonate people or organizations;
- spam or evade platform controls;
- make contracts or financial commitments without required authorization;
- move money.

## Evidence

Marketing evidence and financial evidence remain separate. Every commercial claim must be traceable to its appropriate evidence chain.
