# Brain Money Opportunity Engine

## Purpose
Rank real earning opportunities for Jordan-based execution without treating opportunities as revenue.

## Hard gates
- Jordan eligibility must be explicit or reliably verifiable.
- Remote execution.
- No upfront payment required.
- Clear deliverable and payment terms.
- Current/open listing.
- Legitimate employer/platform.
- No guaranteed-income claims.
- Never record an opportunity as confirmed revenue.

## Scoring
Opportunity Score =
(expected_pay * acceptance_probability * brain_assistance)
/
(time_hours + entry_friction + risk)

Where:
- expected_pay = advertised or conservatively estimated compensation.
- acceptance_probability = 0..1 estimate; never presented as fact.
- brain_assistance = 0..1 estimate of how much Brain can reduce preparation/execution effort.
- entry_friction = normalized cost of applications/tests/onboarding.
- risk = normalized uncertainty around eligibility, payment, or legitimacy.

## Execution states
DISCOVERED -> VERIFIED -> SHORTLISTED -> APPLICATION_READY -> APPLIED -> ACCEPTED -> IN_PROGRESS -> DELIVERED -> PAYMENT_PENDING -> PAYMENT_VERIFIED

Only PAYMENT_VERIFIED may create a confirmed revenue ledger entry.

## Initial opportunity classes
1. Arabic AI evaluation / quality review
2. Arabic AI data annotation
3. Arabic UGC/video recording
4. Arabic AI video production
5. Arabic video editing
6. AI safety / red-team evaluation

## Safety
Brain may research, rank, prepare application material, and organize evidence. It must not fabricate credentials, impersonate the user, bypass platform controls, or claim payment without evidence.
