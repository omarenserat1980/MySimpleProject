# BRAIN Public Identity & Limited Liability Policy v1

## Purpose
Define how Brain presents itself publicly while preserving the distinction between the public-facing Brain brand, its legal entity, its beneficial owner, and any human operator.

This policy is an engineering/governance control. It is not a legal opinion and does not guarantee personal immunity from liability.

## Public identity
1. Customer-facing communication should use the registered legal entity and/or approved Brain brand, not an individual's personal identity, where legally permitted.
2. Brain must maintain a public contact channel owned or controlled by the legal entity.
3. Public pages must disclose the legal entity information that applicable law requires.
4. A human operator is not automatically a personal contracting party merely because they operate Brain.
5. Brain must never falsely claim that a legal entity, license, registration, tax status, or authorization exists before verified evidence is recorded.

## Personal-liability safeguards
The system must flag and block, by default:
- personal guarantees;
- personal borrowing for Brain;
- contracts signed in an individual's personal capacity when the intended counterparty is Brain;
- commingling personal and company funds;
- undocumented personal payment of company obligations;
- representations that an individual personally guarantees Brain's debts.

Any exception requires explicit human approval and appropriate legal documentation.

## Beneficial ownership and lawful disclosure
Privacy from the general public is distinct from lawful disclosure. Brain must not be designed to conceal beneficial ownership, controllers, directors, or authorized representatives from regulators, banks, payment providers, tax authorities, courts, or other parties entitled to the information by law.

## Profit
Profit distribution is permitted only after the legal entity's accounting, tax, creditor, contractual, and corporate requirements have been satisfied. Brain must not treat gross receipts as personal profit.

## Evidence gate
The commercial system may transition to PUBLIC_COMMERCIAL_OPERATION only when the legal-entity evidence required for the selected jurisdiction and activity is recorded and approved.

## Required states
PROJECT_ONLY -> ENTITY_PLANNING -> ENTITY_REGISTERED -> COMPLIANCE_READY -> PUBLIC_COMMERCIAL_OPERATION

ENTITY_REGISTERED requires registration evidence.
COMPLIANCE_READY requires the applicable tax, invoicing, licensing, privacy, and payment checks to be completed or explicitly marked not applicable.
PUBLIC_COMMERCIAL_OPERATION requires human approval.

## Non-goals
This policy does not promise zero personal liability. Liability depends on the chosen legal structure, role, conduct, contracts, guarantees, and applicable law. A qualified local lawyer/accountant should review the final structure before registration or commercial contracting.
