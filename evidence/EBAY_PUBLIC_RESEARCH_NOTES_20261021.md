## Interpretation and limitations
- These are five individual public sold-listing examples, not five validated product niches. Each title appears once; one sale cannot establish demand, sales velocity, or a reliable typical price.
- The examples are US-market sales and their displayed shipping is not an estimate of shipping from Jordan. They must not be used as a profit forecast for a Jordan-based seller.
- No unit acquisition cost, eBay fee schedule, international shipping cost from Jordan, packaging, customs, tax, currency conversion, return risk, or payout cost was established. Therefore the analyzer correctly leaves modeled profit as null.
- eBay Product Research is the preferred next source for broader historical sales data and sell-through where available. It requires the user's own signed-in seller access; this execution did not access the user's account.
- Jordan appears on eBay's seller-country list, but seller onboarding and payouts still require the user's real identity and a payout method accepted in their account. This was not verified against the user's account.
- No login, scraping automation, listing creation, purchases, seller messages, paid services, or financial transactions occurred.
