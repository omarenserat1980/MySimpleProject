# CI Repair Sprint — Draft Service Page

**Status: Draft copy for owner review. Not published and not yet validated by customer demand.**

## Fix one bounded CI failure

A focused troubleshooting and repair sprint for one GitHub Actions / Python CI failure. The aim is to leave you with a reviewable change, a regression check, and a clear handoff report—not an unexplained patch.

### Pilot scope

- One repository and one failure class.
- Reproduce or isolate the failure using the evidence and access agreed in advance.
- Explain the likely root cause and propose a focused fix.
- Add or update regression coverage where practical.
- Deliver a branch or pull request, test evidence, limitations, and rollback notes.

### Indicative pilot price

**USD 49–99**, subject to scope review and mutual agreement before work begins. This is a proposed price range, not a quote, contract, or evidence of customer acceptance.

### Not included by default

Production deployment, credentials or secrets rotation, major refactoring, paid infrastructure, ongoing support, and guarantees against future failures.

### What to provide

A repository URL, failing workflow/run URL, expected behavior, acceptance criteria, and deadline. Redact secrets and personal information from logs. Share only access you are authorized to grant.

### How acceptance works

Before work starts, agree on scope, price, payment timing, and exact acceptance checks. At handoff, review the change and test evidence against those checks. Any scope change requires renewed agreement.

### Honest status

This is a proposed service. No customer engagement, completed external delivery, or payment is claimed. Customer names, repositories, logs, and results will not be published without permission.
