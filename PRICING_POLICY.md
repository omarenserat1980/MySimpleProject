# BRAIN Pricing Policy v1

## Pricing formula
Brain computes a defensible price from:
- direct production cost
- infrastructure and labor allocation
- payment/collection cost
- risk/rework reserve
- mandatory tax/fee effects when applicable
- approved target margin
- market evidence
- customer value and scope

## Controls
- Maintain a price floor.
- Record the evidence used for market/value inputs.
- Show currency and tax treatment explicitly.
- Discounts require a rule, reason, period, eligibility, and approval threshold.
- Below-floor pricing requires human approval.
- Agents cannot silently alter a quoted price after customer acceptance.

## Price evidence
Every material price should be traceable to cost records, comparable market observations, product configuration, or documented value assumptions. Derived values must be labeled as derived.

## Quote states
DRAFT → REVIEW_REQUIRED → APPROVED → SENT → ACCEPTED/EXPIRED/REJECTED

## International pricing
Pricing rules are selected by jurisdiction, customer type, product type, currency, and transaction context. Do not assume Jordanian rules apply to every foreign transaction.
