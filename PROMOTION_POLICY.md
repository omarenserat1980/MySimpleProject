# BRAIN Promotion Policy v1

## Purpose
Promotions must be truthful, documented, time-bounded, eligibility-controlled, and auditable.

## Required campaign record
CAMPAIGN_ID, PRODUCT/SCOPE, REFERENCE_PRICE_BASIS, OFFER_RULE, PROMOTIONAL_PRICE_OR_FORMULA, START_DATE, END_DATE, ELIGIBILITY, MARKETING_CONSENT_BASIS, APPROVAL, EVIDENCE.

## Consent separation
SERVICE_CONSENT != MARKETING_CONSENT != ANALYTICS_CONSENT

A customer request for service does not by itself authorize unrelated marketing.

## Discount controls
Discounts must identify the reason and approval level. Reference prices must have an evidence basis; Brain must not manufacture artificial “before” prices.

## Campaign audit
Record audience basis, communication channel, content version, approval, consent basis, start/end dates, and outcome metrics where lawful and appropriate.
